Questions

How does the trial work?

Install Comma and use it freely for 14 days, with every feature. When the trial ends, Comma goes silent until you enter a license key.

How many computers can I use?

One license works on three computers that you own. To move it, open Menu > License and click Deactivate on the old computer.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only once, to activate your key. After that Comma works offline.

Comma says it cannot reach the license server.

Check your internet connection. A firewall, or a line in the Windows hosts file, may be blocking api.lemonsqueezy.com: remove it and try again.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. Comma is Windows only for now (VST3 and standalone).

Where are my presets and sounds?

In Documents\Comma. You can choose another folder (a synced drive, for instance) from the preset browser.

Who handles the payment?

Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our reseller: they send your license key and your invoice by email, and handle VAT.
